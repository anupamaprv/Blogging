Flagship Case Study

AI Career Coaching Resource Hub

Built a content-led website with a blog and embedded AI coaching tool to help career pivoters get structured, personalized guidance without needing expensive 1:1 coaching as the first step.

Problem Career pivoters have fragmented advice, low confidence, and no easy way to turn generic tips into a plan.
Product role demonstrated Discovery, positioning, persona definition, feature prioritization, AI UX, launch messaging, and iteration.
Artifacts included Personas, competitor notes, MVP scope, prompt design, success metrics, risks, roadmap, and lessons learned.
USER PERSONA

“The Experienced Pivoter”

12+ years in IT or operations, strong delivery background, but needs help translating work into product language.

AI COACHING TOOL

Prompted career guidance

Users answer a few structured questions and receive portfolio, resume, and interview positioning guidance.

NEXT ITERATION

Better activation loop

Add portfolio templates, saved answers, and email capture after the first valuable coaching response.

Sample teardown structure

Recruiters do not need a dissertation. They need crisp evidence that the candidate can reason like a PM.

1 What user/job is this product solving for?
2 What business metric does the feature likely move?
3 What would I improve, and what tradeoff would I accept?

AI experiment proof points

Use the AI tool to show product judgment, not just prompt-writing. The key is trust, usefulness, and repeatability.

UX How users enter context and receive useful, non-overwhelming output.
Risk Hallucination handling, disclaimers, escalation paths, and content boundaries.
Eval How answer quality, completion rate, and return usage would be measured.
